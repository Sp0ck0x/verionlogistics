Why the Baltics

A compact market that rewards a local partner.

What we look for

Manufacturers we want to hear from.

We represent a small number of European manufacturers at a time, so each partner gets real attention. We look for durable B2B products where local documentation, specification and delivery make the difference.

A good fit

  • B2B products sold through projects, contractors, dealers or tenders
  • Clear technical documentation, and certificates where required
  • No current representation in the Baltic states
  • Able to supply within the EU on agreed lead times

Not right now

  • Food, chemicals and hazardous goods
  • Consumer retail products
  • Excise or licensed products
  • Products without verifiable documentation

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Which markets do you cover?

Latvia first, with Lithuania and Estonia as agreed with each partner. Our base is in western Latvia.

Do you require exclusivity?

No. We start non-exclusive. Exclusivity for Latvia or the Baltic states can follow once results are agreed together.

We already sell to the Baltics directly. Why add a partner?

Direct sales reach the buyers who already know you. We work on the ones who don't: local designers who write specifications, contractors bidding public tenders, and municipalities that need documents in Latvian.

How are terms agreed?

Each partnership is set out in a written agreement covering pricing, delivery, payment, warranty and returns, tailored to your products.

Will you use our brand and images?

Only with your written permission, and only to sell your products. Your brand stays yours.

Who handles warranty and technical questions?

We are the customer's first point of contact and coordinate with your technical team, following the warranty terms agreed with you.

What do you need to start?

A product overview, technical documentation, distributor terms and your usual delivery terms. That is enough for us to judge the fit and reply.
